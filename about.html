Lyari Education Journey

Hunain Abbas

At 16, Hunain started street schools in Lyari. That first step grew into a lasting commitment to education and community-led change.

He later adopted a government school and established the area’s first Entrepreneurship Academy. Through the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy, young people and single mothers can access free IT and start-up learning.

His experience spans HR relations, the Navy League, Feroze1888, Fulcrum Pvt Ltd, Al-Sons, HRSI and Borque Cooperation. As President of the Rotaract Club of Karachi Sunset Millennium, he has encouraged young people to build skills and serve their community.
